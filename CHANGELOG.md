# Changelog

All notable changes to Cribbage are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added
- First release of Cribbage: A Lighthouse Card Game for Windows, a native game written in Go.
- Full cribbage rules: the deal alternates, everyone lays away to the dealer's crib, the starter is cut (a jack is "his heels" for 2), then pegging to 31 with fifteens, pairs, pair royals, runs, "Go" and the last card. The show counts fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes and "his nobs", hands first and the crib last. Play to 121 or 61, with skunks and double skunks.
- Two, three or four players; four play as partners across the table.
- A wooden cribbage board in the corner with a front and back peg for each side, and a tally box that adds up every hand as it's counted.
- Single player against the cottage regulars, made up fresh for every game. Each has their own portrait and a name that fits their face (Nana Ruth, Cap'n Eli, Skipper Mags, Keeper Jeb and many more) and their own way of talking.
- Play Together on the same computer: 2 to 4 people take turns, with a "pass the computer" screen so nobody sees anyone else's cards. Regulars fill the empty chairs.
- Play Together online: host a table or join a friend's by address, on your home network or over the internet. Everyone plays from their own seat, and if someone leaves, a regular takes over their cards.
- A hand-drawn harbour deck: the Keeper, the Captain, the Lobsterman and the Boatwright for kings; the Pie Baker, the Quilter, the Captain's Lady and the Gardener for queens; the Deckhand, the Clam Digger, the Ferryman and the Fiddler for jacks. The card back shows Seal Harbor Light.
- A scrubbed pine table in the keeper's cottage, with a mug of cocoa and a brass lantern.
- Three original tunes: "The Keeper's Reel" (fiddle and guitar), "Fog on the Bay" (concertina air) and "The Lobster Pot Jig", with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects for shuffling, dealing, laying away, cutting, pegging, "Go", fifteens, runs, pair royals, big hands and nineteen (a hand worth nothing).
- Choice of pace: Easy, Regular or Quick.
- Saved record: games played and won, skunks, hands counted, your best hand and any perfect 29s.
- Credits & Changelog screen, How to Play guide, and the version number on the table and in the title bar.
