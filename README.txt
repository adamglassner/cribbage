CRIBBAGE - A Lighthouse Card Game  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
=============================================

Open Cribbage from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. Or just double-click Cribbage.exe.

HOW TO PLAY
- Two or three players each get their own score; four play as partners
  sitting across from each other.
- Deal: 6 cards each with two players, 5 each with three or four (with
  three, one card also goes to the crib). Everyone lays cards away into
  the crib so that each player keeps 4. The crib belongs to the dealer.
- The starter: the top of the pack is turned up. A jack is "his heels"
  and the dealer pegs 2.
- Pegging: take turns playing a card, adding to a count that can't pass
  31. Score 2 for making 15 or 31, 2 for a pair, 6 for three of a kind,
  12 for four, and 1 a card for a run of three or more. Say "Go" when
  you can't play; the last player to lay a card pegs 1.
- The show: each hand is counted with the starter (fifteens 2 each,
  pairs, runs, a flush of 4 or 5, and 1 for "his nobs": the jack of the
  starter's suit). The crib is counted last, for the dealer.
- First to 121 (or 61) wins. Finish below 91 (or 31) and you're skunked.
- F2 = new game, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen.

WAYS TO PLAY
- Deal: you against the cottage regulars, new ones every game.
- Play Together > Same Computer: 2 to 4 people share this PC. Before each
  person's turn the game asks you to pass the computer over, and cards
  turn face down in between.
- Play Together > Online: one person picks Host, the others pick Join and
  type the host's address (shown on the host's screen). Empty seats are
  filled by cottage regulars. Online games use port 47261; across the
  internet the host may need to forward that port on their router.

Your record and settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\Cribbage,
outside the install folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All card art, music and sounds are original and made by Ironite.
